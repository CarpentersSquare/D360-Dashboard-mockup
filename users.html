Users

Manage user accounts, roles and team assignments
Manager role and above. Users is the full company roster of every active user account — Managers and Admins use it to add new users, and set or amend a user's role level (Agent, Trainer, Team lead, Manager, Admin) along with any individual access overrides from their profile drawer. Live status and performance data lives on the Call Centre Dashboard, QA Review and My Team Performance instead. Team leads get their own scoped roster on My Team Performance; hidden here, along with Trainer and Agent roles, in the sidebar's "Viewing as" switcher. Not enforced — this prototype has no real authentication or RBAC; see Team roles for the role definitions.
