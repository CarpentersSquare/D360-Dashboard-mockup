Customer Simulations v1.2.0
Prototype
Trainer role and above. Customer Simulations lets trainers and team leads rehearse tricky inbound and outbound scenarios with agents using simulated customer personas before they go live. Not enforced here — this prototype has no real authentication or RBAC; see Team roles for the role definitions.
