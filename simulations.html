Customer Simulations

Rehearse inbound and outbound calls with simulated customer personas
Trainer role and above. Customer Simulations lets Trainers, Team leads, Managers and Admins rehearse tricky inbound and outbound scenarios with agents using simulated customer personas before they go live — hidden from the Agent role in the sidebar's "Viewing as" switcher. Not enforced here — this prototype has no real authentication or RBAC; see Team roles for the role definitions.
