Customer Simulations lets Trainers, Team leads, Managers and Admins rehearse tricky inbound and outbound scenarios with agents using simulated customer personas before they go live — hidden from the Agent role in the sidebar's "Viewing as" switcher. Not enforced here — this prototype has no real authentication or RBAC; see Team roles for the role definitions.
