Templates

Response templates the AI suggests to agents during interactions
Trainer role and above. Templates is the shared library of ready-to-use responses agents are offered during email and live chat interactions — Trainers, Team leads, Managers and Admins can add new templates and amend the wording here as processes change. Hidden from the Agent role in the sidebar's "Viewing as" switcher. Not enforced — this prototype has no real authentication or RBAC; see Team roles for the role definitions.
