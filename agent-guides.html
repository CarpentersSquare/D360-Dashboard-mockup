DPA Verification

Begin the interaction
Inbound Call
Outbound Call
Live Chat
Provide greeting to the customer and offer assistance.
DPA must be completed on every single interaction that you take.
Internal Information Only

You are required to ask these 3 mandatory questions in order for a customer to pass DPA:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Last 4 digits of SSN

If for any reason you need to ask the customer further questions in order to locate an account, the other questions you may ask are as follows:

  • Email address
  • Full address
NEVER provide any information regarding an account before DPA is successfully passed by the account holder. Compliance fail if you do!
DPA complete — Inform the Customer:
"Thank you for confirming your information with me {{first_name}}. Just to confirm, you called in today regarding {{reason}}?"
Continue with the relevant process based on the reasoning for the call

Password Reset Verification

Customer requests a password reset or account unlock
Full DPA verification is required before any reset action is taken.
Internal Information Only

Confirm all 3 mandatory DPA questions before continuing:

  • Full name
  • Date of birth
  • Last 4 digits of SSN
NEVER reset a password or unlock an account before DPA has passed. Compliance fail if you do!
Send the reset link to the verified email address already on file — never a new or updated email.
Confirm receipt with the customer before ending the interaction.

Dispute Rights & Validation Notice

Customer is discussing an outstanding balance or repayment
The dispute rights & validation notice must be given before the call ends.
Internal Information Only

Read the notice using the approved wording — do not paraphrase:

  • The debt will be assumed valid unless disputed within 30 days
  • The customer can request the name and address of the original creditor
  • Any dispute must be provided in writing
Missing this notice is a mandatory compliance fail regardless of the rest of the call. Compliance fail if you do!
Log that the notice was given in the call notes before wrap-up.

Confidentiality & Third-Party Disclosure

Someone other than the account holder is on the line
Confirm they are an authorised 3rd party before discussing anything account-specific.
Internal Information Only

You are bound by the Company's Confidentiality Agreement on every call:

  • Never confirm whether someone has an account with us to an unverified caller
  • Never discuss balance, payment history or personal details with an unauthorised 3rd party
  • Authorised 3rd parties must still be verified against the account before any details are shared
NEVER disclose account information to an unauthorised third party. Compliance fail if you do!
If unsure, take a message and arrange a callback to the account holder directly.

Call Recording Disclosure

Begin the interaction
Recording disclosure must be given in the opening seconds of every call.
Internal Information Only

Use the approved wording at the start of the call:

"This call may be recorded and monitored for quality and training purposes."
Skipping this disclosure is a compliance fail, even if the rest of the call goes well. Compliance fail if you do!
Continue with the greeting and the reason for the call.

Tone & De-escalation

Customer becomes frustrated, raises their voice, or pushes back
Stay calm, slow your pace, and acknowledge how they feel before you respond.
Internal Information Only

Keep it professional even when the customer isn't:

  • Never match a raised voice or talk over the customer
  • Never use threatening, profane or misleading language — this is an automatic QA fail
  • Offer a concrete next step rather than an apology alone
NEVER raise your voice, use profanity, or mislead a customer to end a call faster. Compliance fail if you do!
If the customer stays escalated, follow the Complaint Handling & Escalation guide.

Complaint Handling & Escalation

Customer raises a complaint
Acknowledge the complaint — do not get defensive or argue.
Internal Information Only

Before ending the call you must:

  • Log a complaint reference number
  • Summarise the issue back in the customer's own words
  • Confirm next steps and a timeframe
Send the Complaint acknowledgement template if the complaint is by email.
If it's outside your authority to resolve, escalate to your Team Lead within 1 hour.
Log the outcome and continue with the relevant process.

Callback Booking

Customer requests a callback
Internal Information Only

You must capture all 3 of the following before booking:

  • Best contact number
  • Preferred callback window
  • Reason for the callback
Read the details back to the customer to confirm they're correct.
Log the callback in the system before ending the interaction.
Confirm with the customer:
"All booked, {{first_name}} — one of our team will call you on {{phone}} at {{callback_time}}. You'll get a text reminder beforehand."

Call Wrap-up Checklist

The call, chat or email has ended
Internal Information Only

Before moving to your next interaction, confirm:

  • The AI-generated summary is accurate
  • Any validated actions (refunds, resets, callbacks) were logged
  • The outcome is tagged correctly
Wrap-up should be confirmed within 30 seconds of the interaction ending.
You're ready — move to the next interaction in your queue.
